The first transaction of every block sends the reward to the account number its miner chose. The reward is newly created coins plus the fees of the cheques in that block.

Blocks come every minute. For a fair start, the first year (525,000 blocks) mines 4 coins a block: 2,100,000 coins, 10% of the supply, so people have time to hear of it and join. After that each block mines 4.5 coins, halving every 2,100,000 blocks, about every four years, so each of years 2 to 4 mines more than the first. The total can never pass 21,000,000.

A reward can be moved only after a waiting period, so coins from a block that gets replaced never move. A miner may also mine to nobody: those coins are then never created.
