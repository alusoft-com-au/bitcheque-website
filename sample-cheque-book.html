Sample cheque book

This is a sample made from the public practice seed shown on the Bitcheque site. Anyone can recreate it, so it is for practice only.

  1. One 24-word seed makes the whole book. It is printed as two parts in separate print runs.
  2. Part A (pages 1–11) holds the recipient columns P1–P12 of cheques 0–10.
  3. Part B (pages 12–22) holds the amount D1–D4, exponent E and fee F columns.
  4. For a real book, print on a clean, offline computer with a USB-only printer, print part A and part B separately, keep the two parts apart, and switch everything off afterwards.
  5. Each box is one cell: its position (for example P3·5) and its 12 words. To sign, copy the 12 words of the box that matches each digit.
