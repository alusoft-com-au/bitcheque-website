What the states mean

In sync Within 2 blocks of the highest node, on the same chain.

Behind More than 2 blocks behind: catching up, or cut off from the others.

Fork Same height as the others but a different block. A brief fork is normal; a lasting one is a problem.

Down Not answering.

"Blocks have stopped" means no block for six target intervals. One long gap is normal: proof-of-work intervals are exponential, and a wait several times the average happens every day.
